# Patterns whose facts are checked against pcre2test "/I".
#
# One PHP pattern per line, written as PHP reads it, delimited by "/". Lines
# that start with "#" and blank lines are skipped. Every pattern compiles on
# PHP 8.4 with PCRE2 10.49. After a change, regenerate pcre2test.out with
# generate.php (see its header).
